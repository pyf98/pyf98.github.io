I am a Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA, where my research focuses on omni-modal large language models and full-duplex voice agents. I work on post-training, synthetic data generation, and evaluation to advance interactive audio and audio-visual agents, which have been incorporated into NVIDIA’s Nemotron model family, including [Nemotron-Omni](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni-30B-A3B-Reasoning-BF16), [Nemotron-VoiceChat](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-NemotronLabs-VoiceChat-11B), and [Nemotron-Transcribe](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B).

I earned my Ph.D. from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. [Shinji Watanabe](https://sites.google.com/view/shinjiwatanabe), and my bachelor’s degree from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in 2020. At [CMU WAVLab](https://www.wavlab.org/), I led the [Open Whisper-style Speech Models (OWSM)](https://www.wavlab.org/activities/2024/owsm/) project, developing the first large-scale, fully open speech foundation model from academia. I was also a core contributor to [ESPnet](https://github.com/espnet/espnet), one of the most widely used open-source speech processing toolkits. During my doctoral studies, I interned at NVIDIA, Meta FAIR, and ASAPP.

I have published papers at top-tier venues and received the following awards:
- [INTERSPEECH 2025 Best Student Paper Award (first-authored)](https://www.interspeech2025.org/best-student-papers)
- [EMNLP 2024 Best Paper Award](https://2024.emnlp.org/program/best_papers/)
- IEEE SLT 2024 Best Paper Award
- [ICASSP 2023 Top 3% Paper Recognition (two first-authored and one co-authored)](https://2023.ieeeicassp.org/top-3-percent-paper-recognitions/)
- SPIE Medical Imaging 2020 Best Student Paper Award Finalist (first-authored)
